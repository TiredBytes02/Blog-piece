This website contains the blog posts I have created as part of the Creative Technologies Professionalism module.

As a Games Development student, I will be exploring topics such as professionalism, networking, freelancing, online presence and intellectual property within the games industry. These are all areas that I may encounter in my future career, making them important topics to understand. Bethesda Game Studios and the Fallout Franchise

Why Bethesda Game Studios?

For this project, I decided to use Bethesda Game Studios and the Fallout series as a recurring case study throughout my blogs. Bethesda is one of the most well known game studios in the RPG genre and is responsible for some of the most influential games released over the last two decades, including The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion, The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim, Fallout 3 and Fallout 4. I think Bethesda is an interesting studio to look at because it shows both the opportunities and challenges that come with developing large scale games.

As a major game developer, Bethesda provides valuable examples of how studios manage large scale projects,build and maintain communities, market their products and support games after release. Their work provides useful examples of community management, marketing, project development and supporting games after release.

Why Fallout?

I've chosen Fallout because it's one of my favourite game franchises and because it offers plenty of examples that relate directly to the topics covered in this module. Games such as Fallout 3, Fallout: New Vegas and Fallout 4 are often praised for their world building, player freedom and memorable stories. They have also built large and dedicated communities that remain active years after release through discussion, content creation and modding. The series also shows that game development is not always straightforward. Fallout 76 had a difficult launch and received significant criticism from players.

However, Bethesda continued to support the game with updates and improvements over several years. This makes it a useful example when discussing professionalism, communication with players and how studios respond to mistakes. While Fallout will be the main focus of this website, I will also refer to other games, studios and developers where relevant in order to explore wider issues within the creative industries.
