Through my studies, I have gained experience in various aspects of game development, including programming, design, and project management.

Why this topic interests me

As someone studying Games Development, I have always been interested in how game studios build successful franchises and maintain long term relationships with their players. While creating a game is a major achievement on its own, keeping players engaged for years afterwards is an entirely different challenge. The Fallout series provides some great examples of this. From the lasting popularity of Fallout 3 and Fallout New Vegas to the huge modding communities that surround Fallout 4, the franchise continues to attract players years after release. At the same time, Bethesda's handling of Fallout 76 demonstrates how studios can respond to criticism and work to improve both their products and their relationship with players. By using Bethesda and Fallout as a recurring case study throughout this website, I hope to explore how professional skills such as communication, networking, reputation management and community engagement play an important role within the games industry.
